# RailAssist – Future Enhancements

## Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Vision

RailAssist is designed to evolve into a comprehensive Railway Retirement Management Platform.

The current implementation focuses on retirement settlement calculations and benefit advisory.

Future versions may extend to broader HR and pension management capabilities.
