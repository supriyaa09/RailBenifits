# CGEGIS Settlement Rules

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Rule ID:** RULE-CGEGIS-003

**Status:** Draft

---

# Purpose

This document defines the business rules governing the settlement of benefits under the Central Government Employees Group Insurance Scheme (CGEGIS) for Railway employees.

The settlement engine determines how the calculated CGEGIS benefits are processed, verified, and included in the employee's final settlement package or paid to the eligible beneficiary.

This document contains business rules only and does not perform benefit calculations.

---

# Scope

These rules apply to:

- Superannuation Retirement
- Voluntary Retirement
- Compulsory Retirement
- Invalid Retirement
- Death While in Service
- Other settlement cases permitted under applicable Government rules

---

# Authority

These rules shall be maintained only from official notifications issued by:

- Government of India
- Ministry of Finance
- Ministry of Railways
- Railway Board

# Settlement Workflow

The settlement engine shall perform the following steps:

1. Verify employee eligibility.
2. Obtain the calculated CGEGIS benefit.
3. Validate beneficiary details.
4. Verify applicable Government notification.
5. Determine the payable amount.
6. Generate the settlement record.
7. Forward the benefit for inclusion in the final settlement package.

---

# Business Rules

The settlement engine shall:

- Verify that CGEGIS eligibility has been established.
- Use the benefit amount generated by the CGEGIS calculation engine.
- Determine the rightful recipient based on applicable Government rules.
- Include the benefit in the employee's settlement package.
- Maintain a complete audit trail.
- Preserve historical settlement records.

No benefit calculation shall be performed within this document.

---

# Inputs Required

The settlement engine may require:

- Employee ID
- Employee Group
- Retirement Type
- Date of Retirement
- Date of Death (where applicable)
- Beneficiary Details
- Calculated CGEGIS Benefit
- Applicable Notification Date

---

# Outputs

The settlement process shall produce:

- Settlement Status
- Beneficiary Details
- CGEGIS Benefit Payable
- Payment Authorization
- Settlement Summary
- Audit Record

# Validation Rules

- Eligibility shall be verified before settlement.
- Benefit amount shall originate from the CGEGIS calculation engine.
- Beneficiary details shall be validated.
- Historical settlement records shall remain immutable.
- Only officially approved notifications shall be used.

---

# Error Conditions

Settlement shall fail when:

- Employee is not eligible.
- Calculated CGEGIS benefit is unavailable.
- Beneficiary details are incomplete.
- Required reference data is unavailable.
- Settlement authorization cannot be completed.
